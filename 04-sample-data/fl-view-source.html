How to Claim Florida Lottery Prizes

Learn how to claim Florida Lottery prizes step-by-step, from small retailer payouts to large jackpot claims at district offices.

Prize AmountClaim Method
Up to $599Claim at most authorized retailers or District Offices
$600 – $250,000Claim at a District Office or by mail
$600 – $1,000,000Claim in person at a District Office
Over $1,000,000 / annuityMust be claimed in person at Lottery Headquarters

Documents You Must Bring

  • Signed winning ticket
  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Social Security number or SSN card
  • Completed Winner Claim Form
  • Additional forms for annuity or lifetime prizes

Step-by-Step: How to Claim Your Prize

  1. Sign your winning ticket — Immediately sign the back of your ticket to protect ownership.
  2. Confirm the amount you won — Use the Check Ticket tool or the Latest Draw Results section.
  3. Choose the correct claim method — Based on prize amount, from retailer payout to Lottery Headquarters.
  4. Complete the required paperwork — Winner Claim Form, W-2G if applicable, direct deposit forms for large prizes.
  5. Submit your claim — In person or by mail (certified mail recommended).

Claim Deadlines You Should Know

  • Draw games: 180 days from draw date
  • Scratch-Off games: 60 days after the game officially ends
  • Mailed claims: envelope must be postmarked by the deadline

Where to Claim: Larger prizes can be claimed at district offices statewide, including Miami, Orlando, Tampa, Jacksonville, Fort Myers, and Escambia. Appointments are recommended for jackpot and annuity prizes.

Taxes & Withholding

Florida does NOT charge state income tax on lottery winnings.

Federal tax rules apply for prizes of $600 or more:

  • Mandatory federal withholding: 24%
  • Additional tax may apply at filing
  • W-2G forms are issued for reportable winnings

Florida Lottery Guide & Player Information

Key information Florida Lottery players should understand, including draw integrity, basic rules, responsible play guidance, and where lottery funds go.

Draw Integrity & Oversight

Lottery drawings in Florida follow strict procedures designed to ensure fairness and randomness. Draw machines are inspected before each drawing, numbered balls are tested and weighed, and results are transmitted only after verification.

Key Florida Lottery Rules

  • Minimum age to play: 18
  • Tickets must be signed to become valid property
  • Damaged or unreadable tickets follow a formal review process
  • Retailers cannot refuse to check a ticket
  • Draw cutoff times vary by game

Responsible Play (Florida Guidelines)

  • Set a weekly budget
  • Understand that odds are fixed
  • Avoid chasing losses
  • Florida's gambling helpline is available for support

Where Lottery Money Goes

A significant portion of Florida Lottery revenue supports state education programs, including scholarships and school enhancements. Each year, hundreds of millions of dollars are allocated to Florida's Education Enhancement Trust Fund.
