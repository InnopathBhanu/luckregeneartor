## 1. Scope and integration boundary

Keep the accepted Florida page unchanged through:

- Florida identity and freshness
- Powerball and Mega Millions
- latest Florida-native result family
- result actions and Share
- inline LotteryCorner AI
- upcoming draws
- ticket-check guidance
- Buy Now and concise claim help

Replace the current lower-page experience beginning at the existing “Updates, history and community” region.

The replacement contains exactly five visible bands:

1. Explore Florida Lottery
2. Latest from Florida
3. Florida guides and answers
4. Florida community
5. Florida resources and player support

Remove the current public presentation of:

- Florida history and tools as a plain link directory
- “not published yet” rows
- a separate jackpot-movement text log
- duplicate Sources and methodology headings
- methodology/editorial-policy essays
- All States as a body section
- heading-only modules
- unavailable boxes
- raw official-resource lists in the middle of the page

The existing Change State control near the top and footer navigation are sufficient for State switching.

---

## 2. Product principles

### 2.1 Reader copy

Source evidence controls whether content may appear. It does not dictate how the page speaks.

Do not show repeated:

- Verified
- Official source
- Source checked
- Under review
- Source status
- research citations
- provenance codes
- governance IDs

Use natural public language.

### 2.2 Content ownership

News, guides, and discussion cards are LotteryCorner-owned content.

Their supporting official sources stay in editorial metadata. A normal reader should not be sent to the original official article from a LotteryCorner card.

External Florida Lottery links appear only in the final resources band or a specific claim/commerce handoff.

### 2.3 State hub role

The State hub completes current result tasks and routes to useful depth. It is not a full encyclopedia, legal guide, archive database, newsroom, and forum all at once.

### 2.4 Community tone

Borrow Lottery Post’s friendliness and continuity, not its prediction-heavy content.

Do not show:

- predictions as official guidance
- hot/cold-number promotion
- lucky-number recommendations
- fake users
- fake replies, views, likes, or activity
- fake “trending” labels

# Band 3 — Florida guides and answers

## Public heading

**Florida guides and answers**

## Intro

**Clear explanations for common Florida result, game, and claim questions.**

## Content label

**LotteryCorner Guide**

## Guide 1

**Title:** Why Midday and Evening results can show different dates  
**Summary:** Midday and Evening are separate Florida games with separate drawings. If today’s later drawing has not happened or has not been published, LotteryCorner continues to show the most recent completed result for that member game.  
**Key takeaways:**
- Each member game keeps its own result date.
- Rows remain in a familiar order.
- A pending draw does not erase the last completed result.
- LotteryCorner never borrows a sibling game’s result.
**Tags:** Florida · Results · Midday · Evening  
**Action:** Read guide  
**Secondary action:** Ask AI about different dates

## Guide 2

**Title:** What Fireball means in Florida Pick games  
**Summary:** Fireball is a separately drawn add-on used with eligible Pick plays. It may replace one of the main Pick numbers for prize matching, but it is not an extra ordinary winning ball.  
**Key takeaways:**
- Fireball is drawn separately.
- It applies only when the ticket includes the add-on.
- Ticket play type and amount still matter.
- It is displayed separately from the main result.
**Tags:** Florida · Fireball · Pick 2 · Pick 3 · Pick 4 · Pick 5  
**Action:** Read guide  
**Secondary action:** Ask AI about Fireball

## Guide 3

**Title:** Powerball, Double Play, and Power Play explained  
**Summary:** The main Powerball result, Double Play result, and Power Play multiplier have different roles. LotteryCorner displays them separately so players can see which numbers and options apply to their ticket.  
**Key takeaways:**
- Powerball is the primary drawing.
- Double Play is a separate secondary drawing.
- Power Play is an optional multiplier for eligible purchases.
- Each item keeps its own label and result context.
**Tags:** Florida · Powerball · Double Play · Power Play  
**Action:** Read guide  
**Secondary action:** Ask AI about Powerball options
